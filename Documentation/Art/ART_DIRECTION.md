# Operation First Light — Art Direction

## Target

Operation First Light is grounded near-future Eastern European military realism. The visual target is a cold, wet dawn at a secured communications facility: forest, low mountains, weathered concrete, exposed steel, service roads, utility structures, and equipment built for practical use rather than spectacle.

Use [FirstLight_VisualTarget.png](FirstLight_VisualTarget.png) as the primary mood and composition reference.

## Visual language

- **Weather and time:** cold predawn/dawn light, overcast sky, damp air, restrained mist, wet surfaces, and subdued reflections.
- **Materials:** weathered concrete, galvanized or painted steel, worn asphalt, dark timber, mud, moss, and dense conifer forest.
- **Palette:** charcoal, gunmetal, muted olive, steel blue, concrete gray, and desaturated forest tones.
- **Accent:** sparse burnt-orange security lamps and warning lights. Warm accents identify entrances, security points, and objectives; they are not general decoration.
- **Architecture:** functional communications and security infrastructure with believable access, maintenance, drainage, and defensive needs.
- **Technology:** recognizable near-future equipment, ruggedized controls, physical switches, cables, antennas, and practical optics. The future should feel incremental.
- **Gameplay readability:** preserve clear silhouettes, tactical cover, sightlines, doorways, patrol space, and two-route decisions. Lighting and clutter must support navigation.
- **UE5 implementation:** favor modular kits, trim sheets, shared material instances, decals, hierarchical instancing, sensible LODs, conservative shadow-casting lights, and scalable fog/reflection settings.

## Avoid

- Glossy science-fiction corridors, armor, or weapons
- Neon holograms and omnipresent emissive displays
- Excessive bloom, lens effects, or crushed low-light visibility
- Oversaturated military green
- Real-world faction names, flags, insignia, or political markings
- Prop, foliage, fog, or decal clutter that hides routes, targets, pickups, or interaction prompts
